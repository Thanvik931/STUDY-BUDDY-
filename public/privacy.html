Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Student Cognitive Memory & Data Collection

Study Buddy stores quiz attempts, topic mastery statistics, and diagnosed cognitive memory items (weaknesses and strengths) in MongoDB Atlas to deliver adaptive tutoring. Password hashes are stored using Argon2id with random salts.

2. GDPR & Data Sovereignty

You retain full sovereignty over your educational data:

  • Right to Inspect & Correct: You can view all active memories in the Cognitive Memory tab and override any diagnosis.
  • Data Portability: You can export your full data dossier as JSON via the "Export Data" button.
  • Right to be Forgotten: You can permanently delete your account and all associated cognitive memories using the "Delete Account" button.

3. Contact & Inquiries

For privacy questions or rights requests, contact us at privacy@studybuddy.internal.
